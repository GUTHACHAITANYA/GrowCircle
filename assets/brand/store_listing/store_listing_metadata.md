## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

### English
```text
GrowCircle connects India's complete fresh produce circle: farmers, wholesale buyers, hostels, hotels, transport drivers, and everyday consumers. Whether you want to know today's mandi price before selling, find a verified buyer for your harvest, or arrange a return-load mini-truck, GrowCircle brings clarity, fair pricing, and transparency to every transaction.

WHY GROWCIRCLE?
• KNOW THE PRICE: Live daily prices from official mandis across Andhra Pradesh and India. Compare minimum, maximum, and modal rates across your 3 nearest mandis.
• DIRECT SELLING: Farmers list produce with fresh crop photos and harvest dates. Verified buyers (hostels, mess, hotels, supermarkets) send direct offers. Zero middleman commissions.
• CALL MASKING: Connect securely through private phone calls without sharing personal phone numbers until deals are accepted.
• ON-DEMAND TRANSPORT: Match your harvested load with nearby verified mini-trucks and goods autos. Transparent guide rates prevent price gouging.
• VOICE-FIRST & MULTI-DOOR ACCESS: Use GrowCircle via web app, Android app, WhatsApp voice notes, or free missed-call IVR phone hotline.
• FARM DIARY: Track field costs, calculate break-even rates per acre, and evaluate your net profit per harvest.

DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY:
Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. You have full control over your data: export your records or delete your account at any time directly from the settings menu.

Customer & Grievance Contact:
Email: support@growcircle.in | Phone: +91 80 4710 3920
```
