**Last Updated**: October 2026  
**Applicability**: GrowCircle ("Farm to everyone, full circle") — Web, WhatsApp Bot, IVR Phone Line, and Mobile Applications.  
**Language Availability**: English, Telugu (తెలుగు), Hindi (हिन्दी), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી).

---

## 1. Our Simple Promise
GrowCircle was built to help Indian farmers, institutional buyers, transport drivers, and everyday consumers connect directly. We believe your information belongs to you. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers, loan sharks, or data brokers.

---

## 2. What Information We Collect and Why

### A. For Farmers and Everyday Consumers Checking Prices
- **Phone Number**: We use this only to send you SMS/WhatsApp price alerts that you specifically ask for, or to connect you to verified buyers.
- **District and Preferred Language**: To show you the 3 nearest mandis and speak or text in your language (Telugu, Hindi, Gujarati, or English).
- **Farm Diary Records**: When you log expenses, yield cuts, or sales, this information is stored privately for your farm management only.

### B. For Institutional Buyers and Shopkeepers
- **Business Name, Address, and Proof**: We collect GSTIN, trade licence, or shop photos to verify that you are a genuine buyer before allowing you to view farmer harvest listings.
- **Encryption**: Your registration documents are encrypted (AES-256) so unauthorized parties cannot view them.

### C. For Transporters and Drivers
- **Licence, RC, and Insurance Details**: To ensure only verified, road-worthy commercial vehicles transport farm produce. We never assign loads to drivers with expired documents.

---

## 3. How We Protect Your Privacy (DPDP Act 2023)
1. **Masked Phone Calls**: When a farmer and buyer agree to speak, GrowCircle uses a virtual call bridge. Neither party sees the other’s personal mobile number until a firm deal is finalized.
2. **Zero Location Tracking**: We do not track your live GPS continuously. We only use your district or farm-gate pickup point during an active trip.
3. **No Financial Custody**: GrowCircle never takes custody of your money, bank account details, or payment card PINs. Deals and freight payments are settled directly between you and the other party.

---

## 4. Your Rights
Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023):
- **Right to Download Your Data**: You can download all your records (profile, listings, farm diary, and trips) anytime in standard CSV or JSON format.
- **Right to Delete Your Account**: If you choose to leave GrowCircle, tap "Delete Account" in the app or call our helpline. We will immediately erase your mobile number and identifying details.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: You can stop receiving daily price messages at any time by texting "STOP" on WhatsApp or selecting Option 9 on the IVR helpline.
