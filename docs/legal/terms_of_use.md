## 2. Who We Are and What We Do
- **A Direct Connection Platform**: GrowCircle provides price transparency and connects farmers, buyers, transport drivers, and consumers.
- **No Commission & No Middlemen**: We do not take cuts on produce sales or dictate commodity pricing.
- **No Financial Custody**: We do not hold escrow accounts, accept payments on behalf of buyers, or guarantee counterparty creditworthiness. All commercial transactions, weighing, and payments happen directly between the farmer and buyer.

## 3. Rules for Farmers
1. **Fresh In-App Photographs**: When listing produce, you must capture live in-app photos of the current lot (whole lot, close-up, opened crate). Uploading old gallery images or misleading photos is strictly prohibited and will result in listing removal.
2. **Quality Grading**: Describe your produce accurately as Grade A, B, or C according to the plain guidelines provided.
3. **Firm Commitment**: Once an offer is accepted and confirmed, you agree to make the agreed volume available on the scheduled harvest date.

---

## 4. Rules for Buyers (Hostels, Colleges, Hotels, Shops)
1. **Verification**: Commercial buyers must upload valid registration proof (GST, FSSAI, or Shop & Establishment Act licence). Unverified accounts cannot view farm locations.
2. **Prompt Inspection and OTP Release**: Upon crate arrival, verify the produce with the transporter. If acceptable, release the 6-digit Delivery OTP to the driver.
3. **Disputes Require Photo Evidence**: If produce arrived damaged in transit, you must capture a clear photo of the damaged crates at the delivery gate before raising an arbitration dispute.

---

## 5. Rules for Transporters and Drivers
1. **Valid Documents**: You must maintain valid Driving Licence, Vehicle Registration (RC), Commercial Insurance, and Fitness Certificate (FC). Vehicles with expired documents will be automatically hidden from load dispatches.
2. **Indicative Guide Rates**: Freight estimates shown on GrowCircle are **indicative guide rates only**, calculated by distance and vehicle type. Final freight terms must be agreed directly between transporter and farmer/buyer.
3. **Safe Transport**: Exercise standard commercial care in securing crates and produce against sun and rain during transit.

## 6. Mandi Price Information Disclaimer
- All mandi prices shown on GrowCircle are sourced directly from official government Agmarknet registries (`data.gov.in`).
- Mandi prices reflect wholesale arrival modal rates and are provided as **market guidance only**. Actual spot farm-gate prices may vary based on lot quality, distance, and local demand.
