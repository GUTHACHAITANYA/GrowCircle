# Village Helper Guide (గ్రామ మిత్ర / గ్రామ సహాయకుల మార్గదర్శిని)
> Handbook for Village Youth, SHG Community Resource Persons (CRPs), and Village Kiosk Operators

---

## 1. Your Mission as a Village Helper
As a GrowCircle Village Helper (గ్రామ మిత్ర), you bridge the technology gap for smallholder farmers who do not own smartphones or cannot read English. You help farmers:
1. Know the real daily mandi price so commission agents cannot cheat them.
2. Photograph and list their harvest lots with genuine quality proof.
3. Facilitate deals with trusted institutions (colleges, hostel mess contractors, hotels).
4. Coordinate verified local transporters to pick up produce directly at the farm gate.

---

## 2. Assisting Non-Smartphone Farmers (Feature Phone Flow)
When a farmer has only a basic keypad phone (e.g. JioBharat, Nokia 105):
1. **Missed Call Setup**:
   - Save the GrowCircle missed call number in their phone contacts under *"మండి ధరలు"* (+91 80004 76924).
   - Teach the farmer to give one missed call every morning at 7:00 AM.
   - The system calls back immediately in **Telugu** and speaks today's rates for Guntur, Tenali, and Vijayawada.
2. **Keypad Navigation**:
   - **Key 1**: Connect to sell produce.
   - **Key 2**: Book a local goods auto or mini-truck.
   - **Key 9**: Repeat rates or leave a voice feedback note.

---

## 3. Photographing Produce (The Mandatory 3-Photo Checklist)
When helping a farmer create a listing on your Village Helper smartphone app:
1. **Photo 1: Whole Lot**:
   - Stand 3 meters back. Capture the entire harvest pile, crates, or tractor bed.
   - Purpose: Proof of total quantity and harvest volume.
2. **Photo 2: Close-up**:
   - Hold camera 30 cm from produce. Ensure good natural sunlight.
   - Focus on fruit/vegetable skin, color maturity, and size uniformity.
   - Purpose: Proves grade (Grade A / B / C) without chemical glossing.
3. **Photo 3: Opened Crate**:
   - Randomly open one crate from the middle of the stack. Photograph top and second layer.
   - Purpose: Builds trust by proving no damaged produce is hidden underneath.
4. **Important**:
   - The app strictly enforces **live camera captures**. Photos must be taken within 24 hours of harvest. Screenshots or forwarded gallery images will be rejected automatically.

---

## 4. Deal Finalization & OTP Handshake
Once an institutional buyer (e.g. Acharya Nagarjuna University Hostel) makes an offer:
1. **Review Price & Volume**: Check that the price per kg exceeds the farmer's break-even price.
2. **Masked Call**: If farmer wants to discuss crate return logistics, initiate the masked call. Neither party’s personal number is shared.
3. **Pickup OTP Handshake**:
   - When the assigned goods auto or mini-truck arrives at the farm gate:
   - Verify that the driver's vehicle registration matches the app.
   - Once all crates are securely loaded, give the **4-digit Pickup OTP** to the driver.
   - **Never give the OTP before the crates are physically loaded on the vehicle.**
4. **Delivery OTP**:
   - The buyer checks the crates at the hostel gate and gives the **6-digit Delivery OTP** to the driver to finalize transit.

---

## 5. Capturing User Feedback
At the conclusion of each deal or weekly mandi visit:
- Ask the farmer: *"ఈ రోజు మండి ధర ఖచ్చితంగా ఉందా? రవాణా సమయానికి వచ్చిందా?"* (Was today's mandi price accurate? Did the truck arrive on time?)
- Submit feedback via the app feedback widget (`POST /feedback`) or guide the farmer to leave a 30-second voice note.
- Your feedback directly trains our matching algorithms and alerts our local grievance desk.
